Smart Revise Bot

A browser console script that fills in Smart Revise answers for OCR GCSE Computer Science (J277) from a CSV of stored answers.

Download answers.csv Download script.js View on GitHub

Setup

  1. Open your browser console by pressing F12.
  2. Paste the contents of script.js into the console and press Enter.
  3. Download answers.csv.
  4. Click Import CSV and choose answers.csv.

Answer coverage

All of Component 2 and most of Component 1, Units 1 and 2 are complete. Coverage grows as the shorter units are added.

Use it wisely

Automating the task doesn't count as revision. Use it to check your work or save time on questions you already know, and still go through the material yourself.
